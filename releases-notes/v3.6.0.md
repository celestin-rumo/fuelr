# v3.6.0 — 2026-09-07

A correction, not a feature — and the kind worth writing down, because it
reverses something v3.4.0 said with some confidence.

## Filling the week invents dishes

v3.4.0 filled a week from your own recipes first and asked a model only for
what was left. That was the wrong reading of the request. Somebody filling a
week wants dishes they **have not had**, and handing back the four recipes they
wrote last month is not an answer to it.

So *Remplir la semaine* and *Cuisiner en une fois* now ask a model for
everything and never propose what is already in the library. The
bag-of-groceries screen keeps the library, because "what do I cook with this
tonight" is a different question, and the honest answer is often a recipe you
already like.

Two things follow, and both are on purpose. **Every fill is billed** — the AI
budget is what bounds these screens now, not your library. And **a refusal has
a name**: when a model cannot answer, the screen says which of four things it
is — a paid plan, a spent month (and that it reopens on the 1st), a feature not
wired up here, or a call that failed. Before, all four became "fewer proposals"
that nobody could account for. A screen that refuses without saying why teaches
you to stop pressing the button.

## What a model invents is marked, and the mark cannot be faked

Every accepted proposal becomes a recipe in your library — a draft, with every
guessed quantity flagged, like an import — and it carries where it came from.
The library shows **Créée par IA** on the row, and you can filter on it.

It is a provenance, not a tag, and the difference is the whole point. The six
tags describe the dish and are ticked by hand in the editor; the day somebody
can tick "created by AI" on a recipe they typed, the marker stops meaning
anything. So it is written by the code that creates the recipe, the editor
cannot send it, and correcting an AI recipe from top to bottom leaves it an AI
recipe. A fact about where it came from, not about what it says now.

Writing one is a single call. The previous two-step — create, then update —
left a titleless recipe behind whenever the second half failed.

## Under it

`recipes.origin` is `TYPED`, `IMPORTED` or `AI`, backfilled from `source_url`,
the one piece of evidence the data already held. The library-search services
are gone; what a batch set shares is still **counted** from the lines a model
wrote, never taken from anything it says about itself, and a set that shares
nothing is dropped rather than dressed up.

## What is left

**No payment provider is chosen**, so subscribing, cancelling and the admin
panel's billing section wait on one decision. **The backups still do not leave
the machine.** **Photos are the missing half of offline.** And the pricing page
names one of the three AI capabilities as paid; the other two — reading a
photographed page, and everything on the planner that invents a dish — are
behind the same plan and not yet on that page.
