# v4.8.1 — 2026-09-09

Three fixes to the wait on *Une idée*, from using v4.8.0.

## The dishes really do arrive one by one

They streamed one by one through every stand-in and arrived together in
production: the provider buffers a tool's input and hands it over in a few
large pieces unless asked not to. It is asked now (`eager_input_streaming`),
so each dish shows up as it is written, with its picture on the way.

## The wait says what it is doing

"2.1 Nous concoctons votre deuxième recette…", then "2.2 Nous réalisons
votre deuxième image…" once it is written — in words, one step at a time,
instead of a count.

## A button that does not spin

The *Proposer des plats* button stays itself; while an ask is in flight it
becomes *Annuler*, which stops the ask and keeps the ideas that had already
arrived.

## What is left

Unchanged from v4.3.0.
