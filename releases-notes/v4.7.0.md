# v4.7.0 — 2026-09-08

The picture is there before you decide.

## Drawn while the model is still writing

Until now a dish got its picture after you kept it. Now it is drawn while
it is still a proposal: as the model finishes a title, the picture starts —
while the next dish is being written — so by the time the week, the batch
set or the ideas from the bag are on screen, most of their pictures are too,
and the rest land within seconds. The proposals show them, small, at the
left of each dish.

Keeping a dish hands the picture over: the draft opens with its photo,
nothing is drawn twice, nothing is paid twice. A proposal nobody kept
keeps its picture for a week and is then swept.

## Fewer ideas from the bag

Since every idea is now drawn as it is written, the bag screen asks for
three rather than five — the library still answers first and for free.
