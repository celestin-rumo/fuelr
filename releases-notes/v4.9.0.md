# v4.9.0 — 2026-09-09

The wait says what it is doing; the answer arrives whole.

## One bar, six steps

Asking for three dishes is six things, not one: writing the first recipe,
drawing its picture, writing the second, and so on. The bar says which one
it is on, in words — "2.1 Nous concoctons votre deuxième recette…", then
"2.2 Nous réalisons votre deuxième image…" — and fills over both halves of
every dish, so it never sits still while a picture is on its way. A sheen
crosses it, because a real count stopped for ten seconds reads as a hang.

## Then everything at once

v4.8.0 put each dish on screen as it was written, and the list filled in
under the reader while the pictures caught up afterwards. It reads badly.
Now the pictures are fetched as their dishes are written — the second one
is drawn while the third is being written, nothing is slower for it — and
the whole answer is laid out in one go: three recipes, three pictures,
together.

Cancelling still stops the ask, and shows what had been written, since it
was paid for either way.
