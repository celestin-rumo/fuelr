One unit.

## A sachet is a unit

Baking powder, vanilla sugar and dried yeast come in packets, and *1 sachet
de levure* was being read as one piece of levure — a count of the wrong
thing. The packet is what the shop sells and what the shelf holds, so it is
what the list has to count.

`sachet` is now the sixth unit, beside `g`, `ml`, `pcs`, `c.à.s` and `c.à.c`,
and it reaches every place the list is closed: the editor's selector, the
import reader (which reads *sachet* and *sachets* as the unit rather than
falling through to pieces), the two model tool schemas and their prompts, and
the nutrition scale — a sachet counts as 10 g, what a packet of baking powder
actually holds, the same order of guess as the piece and the spoon.

It carries no shared work in a batch: a packet in two cakes is bought twice
and opened twice, and nothing about it is prepared once.
