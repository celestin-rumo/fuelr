# v4.3.0 — 2026-09-08

Two things: the profile in two cards and four folds, and the wait while a
model writes shown as what it is working on.

## Two cards open

v4.2.0 opened the profile on a name, a language, and six figures in one
block: age, height, weight, sex, activity, goal. The block read as one big
thing, and the language sat first although nobody changes it. So there are
two cards now, and they hold different kinds of fact.

**Vous** is what is true of the person: first name, date of birth, height,
weight, sex. **Activité et objectif** is the two figures that change and take
the journal's target with them — a goal is three small cards, the same three
as the onboarding, because stacked in one block they read as one thing and
side by side they read as a choice. Everything touched once a year or less
is behind a fold below, in this order: the address, the language, the
password, the link to share. Security keeps the devices.

## One place to say what you weigh

The weight was asked twice — in the figures and in the weigh-in — and two
questions get two answers. The field on *Vous* **is** now a weigh-in: typing
60 there records today's figure, the same row the journal's history shows,
and the profile's weight follows the **latest weigh-in** wherever it was
typed. Writing last month's weigh-in in afterwards does not roll it back. The
offer to "recompute the target" is gone, because there is nothing left to
reconcile.

## A birth date, not an age

An age typed at registration is wrong a year later and nobody comes back to
fix it. The profile keeps a date of birth and the age is arithmetic, today —
the API still answers `age`, derived, read-only.

## Said out loud on the hub

Diet, allergies and what you do not like have been under *Préférences
alimentaires* since v4.0.0 and reach every screen that proposes a dish; the
card now says so, since a rule nobody can find is a rule nobody trusts.

## Counted, not timed

Asking a model for fourteen dinners takes about two minutes, and for two
minutes the screen said *on cherche…* over a spinner. A spinner over two
minutes reads as a hang, and people leave.

The three screens that invent dishes — *Remplir la semaine*, *Cuisiner en
une fois* and *Une idée* — now count along. The backend reads the model's
answer as a stream, and a dish's title closes long before its ingredients
and steps do, so the screen is told *plat 6 sur 14 · Dahl de lentilles* while
the dahl is still being written. The bar is filled from that count and from
nothing else: it never advances on a clock, because a percentage invented
from elapsed time is the one kind of progress that is a lie. Before the first
title there is nothing to count, and the bar pulses rather than pretends.

## The pictures are what you typed

Above the bar, the foods turn: *poulet, carottes* becomes a drumstick and a
carrot, one after the other; once dishes arrive, the picture follows the
title of the one being written. Sixteen foods drawn on the same grid and
stroke as every other icon here, in the three languages the app speaks,
accents and plurals aside — and a word nobody recognises simply draws the
pot. Decoration, and it says so: the panel is one polite live region, so a
screen reader hears the count move and not every turn of the picture.

## Nothing else about the ideas changed

Refusals are still named, allergens are still checked by the code on what
comes back, every fill is still billed once, and the one-piece endpoints
still answer exactly as before — the `/live` ones stream the same object at
the end. A stand-in that answers in one piece is read as before, because the
shape is checked on the content type rather than assumed.

## What is left

Unchanged from v4.2.0.
